# Piece sets

Copied from the Chess Opening Studies iOS app (`Board/Pieces.xcassets`), which takes them from SwiftChessTools.

- **Sashité Merida**: https://sashite.dev/assets/chess/, CC0 1.0 Universal.
- **Art Deco, Brutalist, Origami, Circuit Board, Blueprint and Sports Monochrome**: original generated artwork created for SwiftChessTools, bundled here as normalized transparent PNGs.

Regenerate with `node scripts/study-assets.js path/to/ChessOpeningStudies`.
